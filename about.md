# Animated GD

Makes the level feel alive:

- **Animated portals**: every gamemode, gravity, size, mirror, dual, teleport and speed portal gets a breathing glow, energy ripples and sparks swirling around it like a vortex.
- **Gamemode transitions**: switching gamemode fires a shockwave, sucks in the old form's particles and explodes the new form's, flares the portal, pops the icon in elastically and flashes the screen in the new mode's color. Gravity and size changes get their own smaller effects.
- **Animated orbs**: a pulsing halo, a spinning dashed ring, ripples and orbiting sparks. Hitting an orb punches it with a burst of sparks.
- **Animated pads**: a glow with energy rising off the pad. Hitting a pad squashes and stretches it and fires a jet of sparks.

Everything can be toggled and tuned in the mod settings. The effects are purely visual and never touch hitboxes or gameplay.
